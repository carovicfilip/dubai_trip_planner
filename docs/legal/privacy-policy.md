# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dubai Trip Planner mobile application (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and operated by Filip Carovic, an individual developer based in Serbia ("we", "us", "our").

**In short:**

- The App has no user accounts, and we do not run servers that receive your data.
- Your favorites, trip plans and settings are stored only on your device.
- When you ask the App to create a trip plan, the trip details you entered are sent directly from your device to OpenAI so it can generate the plan.
- The App does not use your location, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information on your device, in the standard app storage of iOS or Android:

- Places you save as favorites
- Trip plans you create, including the trip details you entered (start date, number of days, hotel name or area, and selected interests), the generated itinerary, and any items you add or change
- Your settings: app language and light or dark theme
- Technical flags, such as whether you have completed the introduction screens and whether the App has already asked how you like it
- The most recent weather forecast for Dubai, so it can be shown without downloading it again

This information is not sent to us, and we cannot see or access it. The App does not add its own encryption layer. The information is protected by your device's built-in security, such as your passcode and the operating system's storage protections.

If you use device backups, such as iCloud Backup or Google backup, this information may be included in those backups. In that case, Apple's or Google's terms apply to it.

## 3. Information Sent to Third-Party Services

The App has no server of its own. For some features, it connects directly from your device to the services listed below. As with any internet connection, these services receive your device's IP address and the standard technical information included in internet requests.

### 3.1 OpenAI: AI trip plans

When you ask the App to create a trip plan, it sends OpenAI:

- the trip start date and number of days,
- the hotel name or area you entered,
- the interests you selected, and
- the language of the App.

The App does not send your name, email address, contacts, location or any device identifier. Please do not enter personal information in the hotel name or area field.

OpenAI uses this information to generate the itinerary and sends the result back to your device, where it is saved. OpenAI may keep request data for a limited time under its own policies.

Privacy policy: [https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)

### 3.2 OpenWeather: weather forecast

The App requests the weather forecast for a fixed location in central Dubai. It does not send your location.

Privacy policy: [https://openweathermap.org/privacy-policy](https://openweathermap.org/privacy-policy)

### 3.3 Google Fonts: fonts

The App downloads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time they are needed, then keeps them on your device.

Privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 3.4 App Store and Google Play

At one point, the App asks how you like it. Your answer is neither stored nor sent anywhere. The App only saves the fact that the question was asked. If you choose the happy face, the App may ask Apple or Google to show their standard rating window. Any rating or review you leave there is handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Apple and Google may also give us aggregated statistics, such as the number of downloads, and crash reports if you have allowed your device to share this kind of data with app developers. This information does not identify you to us.

### 3.5 Links

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links in the App open our website in your browser. The website is not part of the App.

## 4. What the App Does Not Do

- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It does not access your location. It does not ask for location permission.
- It does not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not use advertising identifiers.
- We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

## 5. How We Use Information and Our Legal Bases

Because the App does not send your data to us, the information we handle ourselves is limited to:

- **Emails you send us.** We use your email address and message only to reply to you.
- **Statistics and crash reports from Apple and Google.** We use them to fix problems and improve the App.

The information sent to OpenAI, OpenWeather and Google Fonts is sent only to provide the feature you are using.

If the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR or the Law on Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia applies to you, our legal bases are:

- **Providing the service you request**, for sending your trip details to OpenAI to create a trip plan.
- **Our legitimate interests** in running, securing and improving the App, for loading fonts and weather data, for crash reports, and for replying to your emails.

## 6. Data Retention

- **On your device:** until you delete it in the App (for example, by removing a favorite or deleting a trip plan), clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
- **At third-party services:** as described in their own privacy policies.
- **Emails:** only as long as needed to handle your request.

## 7. International Data Transfers

OpenAI, OpenWeather and Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Their privacy policies describe how they protect such transfers.

## 8. Security

The App uses encrypted HTTPS connections to communicate with third-party services. Information stored on your device is protected by your device's security features. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to keep the App safe.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EEA, the UK, Serbia or California, you may have the right to:

- access your personal data,
- correct or delete it,
- restrict or object to its processing,
- receive it in a portable format,
- withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent, and
- lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Serbia, this is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

Most of your data is stored only on your device, so you can view, change and delete it directly in the App. You can also clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Requests sent to third-party services do not include your name or any identifier. For that reason, we usually cannot link any data to you. You can still contact us, and we will help as far as we can.

## 10. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us at [carovic.filip@gmail.com](mailto:carovic.filip@gmail.com), and we will delete it.
